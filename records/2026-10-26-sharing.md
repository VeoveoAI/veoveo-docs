# Sharing with people and groups

2026-10-26 (UTC). Coordinator: Opus. Implementation and deploys by delegated agents; the
qualification below was run by the coordinator. Model calls: only G2, recorded in its own
section.

## What shipped

A repository's owner shares it with a person, by the email they sign in with, or with a
group. Groups and their owners live in Hub's control plane beside the assignments. A
Computer's Lore token names one repository, minted for the repository each Lore request
names, so nothing bounds how many repositories a person reaches. Lore and Entra are
unchanged. The design is in Hub's, console's and the installation's `DESIGN.md`.

| Console | `b96a5a2` (`sharing`) | `sha256:f765af4a…e0e4e98b` |

| Installation | Bioma `main` up to `a91c99e`; template `sharing` | `vv-lore` gains `refresh.request_scope {header: lore-partition-bin, scope_template: lore-repo-{value}}`; console's policy admits the five new Hub routes |

**OpenShell's part.** A credential refreshed with `oauth2_client_credentials` may declare
`request_scope`. For a request that carries that header, the supervisor asks the gateway
(`MintScopedProviderCredential`) for a token of the scope the header names, minted with the
provider's stored client material, and holds it until `refresh_before` ahead of its expiry.
The token never enters the sandbox. An issuer refusal forwards the ordinary token, so Lore
refuses natively; an unavailable issuer answers 502 without forwarding.

**Hub's part.** One rule reads grants everywhere: a person's actions on a repository are the
union of their own assignment's and those of every group of the tenant they belong to. The
Computer's client issues the owner's login token without a scope and one repository's token
for `scope=lore-repo-<id>`. The renewal of Computers on assignment changes is gone; each
sign-in records the person in `hub.person` with their email.

## Found: Lore credentials expired before their refresh arrived

During S4 a clone failed with "Failed to resolve repository" and Lore logged nothing. The
member's supervisor had logged `credential resolution rejected: credential is expired` at
23:05:21.967, 1.6 s before the gateway's refreshed value arrived.

The cause is upstream OpenShell code that this installation's profile exposes. The gateway's
refresh worker sweeps every 60 s and refreshed a credential only when `next_refresh_at` had
passed, so a mint finishing just after a sweep began was refreshed one sweep late: the
effective lead was `refresh_before − 60 s`, zero for `vv-lore`'s 60 s. The supervisor's 10 s
configuration poll then held the expired value for a few seconds, and the relay answered 500
without forwarding. Requests that carry no repository header, such as `RepositoryGet`, use the
ordinary token and hit it; scoped tokens carry their own expiry and did not. The deployed
`534250aa0` had the same code.

Fixed in OpenShell `ebd6a1c` (`fix(server): refresh provider credentials before their
deadline, not a worker tick after it`): a healthy state is due on the last sweep before its
deadline, a state in retry backoff keeps its not-before time. A regression test fails under
the old rule ("refresh attempted 517 ms before expiry") and passes under the new one.

Deployed as gateway `sha256:1c2b99cd…` (Helm revision 21, 23:42:52 Ready; supervisor and
sandbox unchanged). Before it, the gateway logged `seconds_until_expiry=0 due=true` for every
Lore state. After it, every steady-state Lore refresh was due 60 to 86 s before expiry (four
states already past their refresh time when the new worker started caught up at 26 s). Both
Computers' supervisors logged no expiry rejection from the new gateway's start to 23:50.
Hub's availability during this roll was not sampled; three probes after it answered 200.

## Boundaries

- Two people of one tenant, one group, 200 repositories; not more people, groups or tenants.
- The removal bound (240 s) is the supervisor's scoped-token cache; S3 measured it, S4 did not.
- The factory's use of the member's grant is inferred from its successful build of the
  member's push; the instance's token was not inspected.
- Membership comes from Hub only; Entra groups are not used.
